# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

This policy covers Markd (listed in the Microsoft Store as Markd Day Counter) on Windows and
Android.

Markd stores the occasions, milestones, categories, notes and settings you enter on your own
device, and nowhere else.

## What is collected

Nothing. Markd has no account, no sign-in and no server. It does not collect, transmit or share
personal information, usage analytics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising and no
third-party tracking.

## What leaves your device

Only what you export yourself. Export writes a backup file wherever you choose to save or share
it, and you decide where that goes. A backup contains your occasions, milestones, categories and
settings, plus the name of the device it was made on, so you can tell backups apart. A backup can
be protected with a password, in which case it is encrypted on your device before it is written.

## Notifications

Markd schedules milestone notifications locally on your device. No notification content is sent
to any server.

## Deleting your data

Erase all occasions in Settings removes every occasion and milestone from the device. Uninstalling
Markd from the Microsoft Store or from Android removes all of its data, including categories and
settings. The portable Windows zip keeps its data in a `com.janmesarc.markd` folder under
`%LOCALAPPDATA%`; delete that folder to remove it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.
Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
